Electrical work is the least-audited line in Indian home budgets — because it is quoted in a unit most homeowners never decode: the point. This guide prices 2BHK and 3BHK wiring at 2026 rates, explains the per-point system so any quote becomes checkable arithmetic, and covers the two expensive special cases: old-flat rewiring and the protection hardware no tier should skip.

The Per-Point System (How Electricians Actually Quote)

One point = one electrical outcome: a light, fan, 6A socket, 16A power socket, AC point, geyser point. Rates in 2026: ₹350–650 per point labour-only, or ₹700–1,400 per point with material. Heavy points (AC, geyser — 4–6 sq mm wire, dedicated MCB) price at the top; light points at the bottom.

This turns any quote into arithmetic: count the points on the electrical layout, multiply, compare. A 2BHK layout showing 85 points quoted at ₹1.9 lakh is being billed ₹2,235/point — ask why. One showing ₹55,000 is being wired with something you don't want in your walls.

Protection Hardware: The Non-Negotiable ₹8–20K

Whatever the switch tier, the distribution board is where safety lives: MCBs sized per circuit, an RCCB (residual current device — the one that prevents electrocution), and proper earthing verified at the earth pit. Fitted cost ₹8,000–20,000 for a 2–3BHK. A quote that shows premium switches but a bare-minimum DB has the priorities exactly backwards — switches are jewellery, the DB is the seatbelt.

Rewiring an Old Flat: +15–25%

Rewiring costs more than wiring: chasing conduits into old, hard, finished walls; abandoning or pulling dead wiring; working around furniture and occupants; and the hidden line — making good every chased wall (putty ₹10–16/sqft + emulsion ₹12–20/sqft, per our Painting Cost Calculator bands). If the flat still runs aluminium wire, has crumbling insulation, or lacks earthing, treat rewiring as a safety project and do it before, not after, the cosmetic interior spend — the sequencing logic is in our flat renovation guide.

Quote Red Flags

  • No point count. A lump sum with no point schedule cannot be compared or audited.
  • Wire brand unspecified, or “equivalent”. Specify brand + gauge + FRLS in writing; verify on the boxes at delivery.
  • No RCCB in the DB schedule. The cheapest dangerous saving in the trade.
  • 20%+ below the band. Thinner gauge, non-ISI wire, or points that will “turn out extra” mid-project.
  • Making-good excluded. Chased walls must be re-plastered and painted by someone — establish who, in the contract.

Bottom Line — Wiring in 2026

  • 2BHK ₹70K–1.5 L, 3BHK ₹1.1–2.2 L at standard spec; per point ₹700–1,400 with material.
  • Count points from the layout, multiply, compare — the quote is arithmetic, not mystery.
  • Freeze the point list before the quote; mid-project points cost full rate with zero leverage.
  • DB + MCB/RCCB (₹8–20K) before premium switches, always.
  • Old-flat rewiring: +15–25%, plus making-good — and do it before the interiors, not after.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the electrical wiring cost for a 2BHK flat in 2026?+

A full concealed wiring job for a 2BHK (70–100 electrical points) costs ₹70,000–1.5 lakh in 2026 at standard specification — FRLS copper wire (Polycab/Finolex/Havells/KEI), ISI modular switches (Anchor/Goldmedal class), conduiting, and a distribution board with MCB/RCCB protection. A 3BHK (100–150 points) runs ₹1.1–2.2 lakh. Premium switch ranges (Legrand/Schneider) and home-automation provisioning push totals 20–40% higher.

What is a 'point' in electrical wiring and what does one cost?+

A point is one electrical outcome — a light, fan, 6A socket, 16A power socket, AC point, geyser point, or switchboard position. Electricians quote per point: ₹350–650 labour-only, or ₹700–1,400 per point with material at standard spec. Heavy points (AC, geyser — thicker 4/6 sq mm wire, dedicated MCB) sit at the top of the band; light points at the bottom. Count your points from the electrical layout drawing and the quote becomes checkable arithmetic.

How many electrical points does a 2BHK or 3BHK need?+

A typical 2BHK carries 70–100 points: lights (25–35), fans (5–7), 6A sockets (20–30), 16A power sockets (8–12), AC points (2–3), geyser/kitchen appliance points (5–8), plus doorbell, TV/data and DB positions. A 3BHK carries 100–150. Counts run higher in new interiors (profile lighting, more sockets) — every added point is ₹700–1,400, which is why the point list should be frozen before the quote, not during the work.

What does rewiring an old flat cost compared to new wiring?+

Add 15–25% over new-construction rates. Rewiring means chasing new conduit routes into finished (often harder, older) walls, removing or abandoning old wiring, working around occupants and furniture, and making good the plaster afterward — the making-good and repainting is the hidden line (putty ₹10–16/sqft + emulsion ₹12–20/sqft on every chased wall). If your flat still runs aluminium wiring or has no earthing, rewiring is a safety project, not a cosmetic one.

Which wire and switch brands should I specify?+

Wire: any ISI-marked FRLS copper from Polycab, Finolex, Havells or KEI — specify the brand and gauge (1.5 sq mm lights, 2.5 sq mm sockets, 4–6 sq mm AC/geyser) in the quote so substitution is checkable. Switches: Anchor/GM/Goldmedal at standard tier, Legrand/Schneider/Panasonic at premium — the jump is ₹40–80 vs ₹150–400 per switch position. Protection is non-negotiable regardless of tier: a proper DB with MCBs per circuit and an RCCB for shock protection, ₹8,000–20,000 fitted.

Is copper price movement reflected in wiring quotes?+

Yes — wire is 35–50% of a wiring job's material cost and copper is a globally traded metal, so quotes drift with it quarter to quarter. This is why per-point quotes are only valid for 30–45 days, and why a quote that is 20% below the band usually means thinner gauge, non-FRLS wire, or a brand swap. Check the wire spec on the boxes at delivery, not on the invoice.
