Pehle samjho: plot aur flat, actually kya hote hain?

Plot sirf zameen hoti hai — koi building nahi, koi structure nahi. Plot kharidne ke baad aapko khud apna ghar design karwana padta hai aur contractor se build karwana padta hai. Plot teen types ka hota hai: RERA-approved (developer ne layout approved kiya hua, sabse safe), NA (Non-Agricultural) (government ne residential use ke liye convert kiya hua), aur Agricultural (Agri) (technically sirf farming ke liye, residential construction ke liye NA conversion zaruri).

Flat ek ready (ya under-construction) residential unit hoti hai — aapko na architect chahiye, na contractor deal. Builder ne pehle se structure bana ke diya hota hai. Under-construction flat mein risk hai builder default ka; ready flat mein risk kam hai lekin premium zyada dena padta hai.

Yeh comparison sirf investment return ke baare mein nahi hai — lifestyle, loan eligibility, legal risk, aur cash flow sab cheezein hain jo consider karni chahiye. Chaliye ek-ek karke dekhte hain.

Appreciation: Kaun Zyada Badhta Hai?

Yeh woh sawaal hai jiske liye broker always plot ki tarfedari karta hai — aur woh partly sahi bhi hai. Plot appreciation typically flat se zyada hoti hai, khaas taur pe:

  • Developing areas mein — jahan infrastructure aa raha hai (metro line, highway, MIHAN-type projects). Yahan plot 15–25% CAGR de sakta hai decade ke duration mein.
  • Tier-2 cities mein — Nagpur, Indore, Surat jaisi cities jahan raw land still underpriced hai relative to future potential.

Lekin broker jo nahi batata woh yeh hai: flat ka rental yield hota hai (typically 2–4% gross), plot ka nahi. Toh agar 10 saal ke liye ₹1 crore invest karein, plot mein sirf appreciation count hoti hai. Flat mein appreciation + rent income. Jo plot appreciation ka gap fill karta hai FD/SIP ke comparison mein — exactly woh calculation hamare Plot Viability Calculator mein hoti hai.

Agricultural plot ke baare mein alert: NA conversion ke bina plot ka actual market value significantly discounted hota hai. Jo broker "₹500/sqft mein mila tha, ₹5,000 ho gaya" bolta hai — usmein NA conversion ki cost aur time factor count karna mat bhulen.

Liquidity: Emergency Mein Bech Sakte Hain?

Plot aur flat dono illiquid assets hain — 3–6 mahine lagate hain proper buyer milne mein. Lekin comparatively:

  • Flat zyada liquid hai urban areas mein. Buyer pool badi hai — end-users, investors, NRI sab flat dekhte hain.
  • Plot buyer pool choti hai — typically developers, investors, ya log jo self-construction karna chahte hain. Ek mature residential neighborhood ke plot exception hote hain — wahan liquidity better hoti hai.
  • Agri plot sabse illiquid hai — non-farmer ko legally buy karna mushkil hai kai states mein (Maharashtra mein non-farmers ko agri land nahi khareedna chahiye bina permission ke).

Maintenance: Flat Mein Society Ka Jhanjhat, Plot Mein Khud Ka

Flat mein maintenance society handle karti hai — security, garden, common area cleaning, elevator. Aap monthly maintenance dete rehte hain (metro cities mein ₹2,000–10,000+). Aapko kuch karne ki zarurat nahi. Disadvantage: aapka control simit hai.

Plot pe aapka full control hai — design se lekar material quality tak sab aap decide karte hain. Lekin boundary wall, security, paani ka connection, bijli ka MSEB/BESCOM connection — sab khud karwana padta hai. Vacant plot pe maintenance cost negligible hai; lekin agar plot par koi construct nahi hai toh encroachment ka risk bhi hota hai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Plot kharidna zyada faydemand hai ya flat 2026 mein?+

Dono ke apne faayde hain. Plot long-term mein zyada appreciate karta hai — khaas taur pe developing areas mein — aur construction pe full control milti hai. Flat tatkaal rehne layak hota hai, home loan easier milta hai, aur maintenance society handle karti hai. Agar 5–10 saal ka horizon hai aur khud banana chahte hain, plot better hai. Agar ghar immediately chahiye ya Mumbai/Bangalore jaisi city mein hai jahan construction costly ho, flat sensible choice hai.

Plot loan aur home loan mein kya fark hai?+

Plot loan sirf zameen kharidne ke liye hota hai — banks generally 70–75% LTV dete hain (vs home loan ka 80–90%), interest rate 0.5–1% zyada lagta hai (May 2026 mein typically 8.5–9.5% vs home loan ke 7.75–8.75%). Plot loan pe Section 24 ka tax benefit nahi milta jab tak construction complete na ho. Aur agar plot agricultural category mein hai, toh loan milna aur bhi mushkil ho jata hai.

Plot ka stamp duty flat se zyada kyun hota hai?+

Actual stamp duty rate same hoti hai — state-wise fixed percentage (jaise Maharashtra mein 5–7%, Gujarat mein 4.9%). Lekin plot pe GST nahi lagta. Under-construction flat mein 5% GST lagta hai. Ready flat mein stamp duty waisa hi hota hai. Toh overall plot ka upfront cost flat se thoda kam ho sakta hai — sirf isliye ki GST ka burden nahi hota. Hamare Stamp Duty Calculator mein exact figures calculate karein.

Agricultural plot kharidna safe hai 2026 mein?+

Agricultural plot (Agri) kharidna risky hai agar aap residential construction chahte hain. Agri plot pe house banana technically illegal hai jab tak NA (Non-Agricultural) conversion na ho. Conversion time le sakti hai — kuch states mein 6 months, kuch mein 3–4 saal bhi. Banks agri plot pe residential construction ke liye loan nahi dete. Sirf tab buy karein agar conversion already done hai ya agar aap purely long-term land investment ke liye khareed rahe hain aur construction ka koi immediate plan nahi hai.

Plot pe construction karna expensive hai ya flat kharidna?+

Combined total typically comparable rehta hai, lekin timing alag hai. Plot ke baad standard quality construction ₹1,500–2,200/sqft mein hoti hai. Ready flat ka carpet area rate effectively ₹4,000–12,000/sqft (city aur location pe), lekin usme chhupa hua builder profit, common area loading, aur amenity cost hoti hai. Self-construction mein aap sab choices khud karte ho aur quality zyada milti hai same budget mein — lekin time aur supervision aap lagate ho.
