Things this calculator does NOT include

  • GST on under-construction property — additional 5% (1% for affordable housing). Ready-to-move properties with OC are GST-exempt.
  • Society transfer fees — typically ₹25,000–2,00,000, paid to the housing society.
  • Lawyer / document fees — ₹5,000–25,000 depending on city and complexity.
  • Bank processing fees if you're taking a home loan — typically 0.25–1% of loan amount.
  • TDS — buyer must deduct 1% TDS on property value above ₹50 lakh and deposit with the IT department.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Maharashtra show 6% but my agent quoted 5%?
Maharashtra municipal areas charge 5% base + 1% Metro Cess (Mumbai/Pune/Thane/Nashik/Nagpur). Outside these metros, the cess doesn't apply, so you pay 5%. The female concession reduces the base by 1%.
Does Karnataka give a women's discount?
No. Karnataka does not differentiate stamp duty by gender as of 2026. The 3% slab applies only to properties priced ₹21–45 lakh. Above ₹45 lakh, both male and female buyers pay 5%.
Joint registration — what rate applies?
In Maharashtra, Delhi, UP, Haryana, Rajasthan and Punjab, joint registration with at least one female owner qualifies for the female rate. Other states (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, etc.) don't differentiate, so joint registration pays the standard rate.
Can I include stamp duty in my home loan?
Most lenders fund up to 90% of property value but only 75–80% of "property value + stamp duty + registration." So you must arrange the down payment + stamp duty + registration in cash. For a ₹75 lakh property in Mumbai, this is roughly ₹15 lakh down + ₹4.5 lakh stamp duty + ₹30K registration = ~₹20 lakh out of pocket.
Is stamp duty refundable if I cancel the deal?
Partially. If the document was not registered, you can apply for a refund of 90–95% of stamp duty within 6 months at your state's IGR office. Once registered, refund is much harder and limited to specific cancelled-allotment cases.

Log Yeh Bhi Poochte Hain (People Also Ask)

Jis tarah se log search karte hain — unke liye jawab

Stamp duty Maharashtra mein kitni lagti hai?
Maharashtra mein male buyer ke liye 6% stamp duty hai (5% basic + 1% Metro Cess) Mumbai, Pune, Thane, Nashik, Nagpur ke metro areas mein. Female buyer ke liye 1% concession hai — total 5% lagta hai. Iske alawa 1% registration fee hai jo maximum ₹30,000 par capped hai. Non-metro areas mein Metro Cess nahi lagta toh effective rate 1% kam ho jata hai.
Mahila ke naam se property registration mein kitni bachat hoti hai?
Bachat state ke hisaab se alag hai. Maharashtra mein 1%, Delhi mein 2%, UP/Haryana/Rajasthan/Punjab mein 1–2% tak ki concession milti hai female-only ya joint registration (with female owner) par. Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Telangana, Madhya Pradesh, Kerala mein gender ke basis par koi concession nahi hai. ₹75 lakh ki property par Delhi mein 2% concession matlab seedha ₹1.5 lakh ki bachat.
Stamp duty home loan mein cover hoti hai kya?
Nahi. Bank typically property value ka maximum 90% loan deta hai, aur woh bhi sirf "property + stamp duty + registration" ke combined cost ka 75–80%. Matlab agar ₹75 lakh ka flat hai Mumbai mein, toh stamp duty (₹3.75 lakh) + registration (₹30K) + 10–25% down payment — sab cash mein arrange karna padega. Total roughly ₹15–20 lakh out of pocket lagta hai before loan starts.
Under-construction property par GST aur stamp duty dono lagti hain?
Haan. Under-construction property par 5% GST (1% affordable housing ke liye) + state ki full stamp duty — dono lagti hain. Ready-to-move property jisko Occupancy Certificate (OC) mil chuki hai, usme GST exempt hai — sirf stamp duty + registration lagta hai. Isliye OC-received ready property roughly 5% sasti padti hai overall tax burden mein, even if listing price same hai.
Agreement cancel ho jaye toh stamp duty wapas milti hai?
Partially. Agar document register nahi hua hai aur 6 mahine ke andar refund apply karte hain, toh state IGR office se 90–95% stamp duty wapas mil sakti hai. Registration ke baad refund bahut difficult hai — sirf cancelled allotment jaise specific cases mein milta hai. Process state ke hisaab se alag hai — Maharashtra mein igrmaharashtra.gov.in par online apply kar sakte hain.
