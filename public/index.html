Why this and not ChatGPT?

AI gives you a paragraph. We give you a number you can act on.

Ask any chatbot “should I buy this 50L plot in Lucknow?” and you get four paragraphs of ‘it depends’. Our calculators give you the all-in cost, the break-even number, and a verdict. Plus the sources, plus a PDF for your spouse, plus a shareable link.

Read the methodology →
1
A verdict, not a hedge
Plot Viability gives green / amber / red on your specific deal. Quote Checker gives fair / high / very high. We compute the break-even number; we don't tell you to ‘consult a financial advisor’.
2
Sourced & dated, not memorised
Every rate cites CPWD SOR, MOSPI WPI, state IGR portals, RBI lender disclosures — with the date last verified. Chatbots can't tell you whether their UP stamp duty number is from 2022 or last week.
3
PDF + share link on every calc
One tap downloads a PDF report you can email to your spouse, contractor, or banker. Or copy a link that pre-fills your inputs — send it to your family WhatsApp group and everyone runs the same numbers.

Common sawaal — seedhe jawab
1000 sqft ghar banane mein kitna kharcha?
Metro city mein standard quality ke liye approximately ₹19–21 lakh (₹1,900–2,100/sqft). Tier-2 city mein yahi ₹14–16 lakh mein ho sakta hai. Exact estimate ke liye upar ka construction calculator use karein.
Contractor ka quote sahi hai ya nahi — kaise pata karein?
Apne city aur quality ke hisaab se per sqft rate compare karein. Metro mein standard quality: ₹1,900–2,100/sqft fair hai. Isse 20% zyada aa raha hai toh 2–3 aur quotes lein. Hamare Quote Checker se seedha check karein.
2 BHK interior kitne mein hota hai?
Metro city mein 2BHK ka complete interior: Basic ₹3.5–5 lakh | Standard ₹7–10 lakh | Premium ₹12–18 lakh. Room-wise exact estimate ke liye Interior Estimator try karein.
50 lakh ke home loan ki EMI kitni hogi?
8.0% rate, 20 saal tenure par monthly EMI approximately ₹41,822. Tenure 10 saal rakhein toh EMI ₹60,663 hogi — lekin total interest ₹27 lakh kam lagega. EMI Calculator mein apne figures daalen.
