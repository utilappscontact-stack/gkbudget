How to measure paintable area at home

If you don't have a laser measure handy, use this rule of thumb:

  • Walls = (perimeter of room × ceiling height) minus 5–8% for door and window cutouts
  • Ceiling area = same as floor (carpet) area
  • Quick rule: total paintable area ≈ 3× carpet area for typical 10ft Indian ceilings. For 12ft+ ceilings, use 3.5×.

Common cost-cutting tricks contractors use

  • 1 coat instead of 2. Looks fine for the first week, then visible patches appear. Always insist on 2-coat in writing.
  • Mixing cheap distemper into emulsion. Saves them ₹4–8/sqft, ruins the finish in 12–18 months. Buy paint yourself if possible.
  • Skipping primer. Putty + paint without primer fails warranty if you ever raise a complaint.
  • Asian Paints sticker on a Birla bucket. Buy from the authorised dealer, not the contractor's "wholesale supplier."

Frequently asked questions

Should I supply paint or let the contractor supply?
Buy paint yourself from an authorised dealer (Asian Paints Colour World, Berger Express, etc.) and pay the contractor labour-only. Saves 10–18% and eliminates the brand-substitution risk. Contractors who refuse this arrangement are usually marking up paint heavily — often a red flag.
How long does painting a 2BHK take?
A 2BHK with putty + primer + 2 emulsion coats takes 5–7 working days for a 2-painter team. Skipping putty saves ~2 days. Drying time between coats is the bottleneck — full process can't be rushed without compromising finish.
Best season to paint your house?
November to February (low humidity, dust manageable). Avoid monsoon entirely — paint won't cure properly, mould risk, putty fails. Pre-monsoon (April–May) is OK for interiors only. Avoid afternoon painting in peak summer in Delhi/North — direct heat on freshly painted exterior walls causes premature cracking.
Does painting need GST?
If you're paying the contractor's labour (most home painting jobs), no GST is applicable on the labour portion below ₹20 lakh annual contract value. Paint material has 18% GST already included in the MRP — so no double-tax. For corporate / commercial painting jobs above ₹20L, full 18% GST applies on the labour portion.

2BHK painting cost 2026 mein kitna lagta hai?
Typical 2BHK (700–900 sqft carpet, ~2,400 sqft paintable surface) ka painting kharcha 2026 mein: Distemper (basic) ₹36,000–52,000 | Acrylic Emulsion ₹52,000–76,000 | Premium Emulsion (Royale, Apex) ₹72,000–1,00,000 | Luxury (Royale Aspira, Berger Silk) ₹1,00,000–1,44,000. Yeh sab putty + primer + 2-coat ke saath hai. Calculator se exact figure milega apne BHK aur paint type ke according.
Paint khud le ke contractor ko sirf labour pay karein, toh kitni bachat hoti hai?
Authorised dealer se paint khud kharidne par 10–18% bachat hoti hai. Contractors typically Asian Paints / Berger ki MRP par 8–15% markup lagate hain. Plus paint khud lene se brand substitution risk khatam hota hai — warna kabhi-kabhi cheap "wholesale" paint mein milawat hoti hai jisse 12–18 mahine baad finish kharab ho jata hai. Best practice: paint khud kharidein, contractor ko sirf labour de.
Asian Paints zyada mehengi hai ya better hai?
Premium tier mein Asian Paints typically Berger se 5–12% mehengi hoti hai, Berger Nerolac/Dulux se 3–8% mehengi. Indian humidity aur monsoon mein 10+ saal ki colour fastness ke independent tests mein Asian Paints aur Berger roughly equal hain. Nerolac/Dulux 5–7 saal achhe rahte hain. Premium tier mein difference real hai. Basic tier mein difference mostly marketing hai. Putty + primer + good labour — brand se zyada important.
Putty zaroori hai painting ke pehle?
Naye walls par putty (Birla White, JK White, Asian Paints TruCare) lagana zaroori hai — surface smooth karne ke liye aur paint ki life badhane ke liye. Cracks waale walls par bhi putty necessary hai. Lekin pehle se smooth aur well-maintained walls par re-painting kar rahe hain, toh putty skip kar sakte hain — sirf primer + 2-coat paint enough hai. Putty skip karne se 10–14/sqft bachat hoti hai par durability kam ho jaati hai.
2-coat painting karne ka kya matlab hai? 1-coat mein bachat ho sakti hai?
2-coat matlab paint ka pura layer dobara apply karna — pehle coat ke 4–6 ghante baad. Industry standard hai aur paint manufacturer ki warranty bhi 2-coat assume karke deti hai. 1-coat lagane par dark colours par streaks dikhte hain, light colours par patches dikhte hain 2–3 mahine mein. Bachat sirf ₹3–5/sqft labour ki hoti hai par finish saaf taur par kharab dikhta hai. Hamesha contract mein "2-coat application" likhwayein.
