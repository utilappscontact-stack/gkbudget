Accuracy of estimates

Construction and renovation costs vary significantly based on:

  • Site conditions (soil type, slope, accessibility)
  • Material availability in your specific region
  • Contractor skill, market reputation, and current order book
  • Design complexity and customisation
  • Local regulations and approval requirements
  • Quarter-on-quarter market price movements (cement, steel, sand)

Our estimates are based on average market rates derived from CPWD Schedule of Rates, MOSPI WPI Construction Index, NBO city data, Steel Mint commodity tracking, and verified contractor quotes. They will not perfectly reflect your specific project. Treat them as planning ranges, not final budgets.
