Which AI tool works best for these prompts in 2026?
For Indian construction, real estate, and home loan questions, all three major chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) handle these prompts adequately. ChatGPT 4-class and Claude 3.5+ tend to be most specific about Indian context (state-wise stamp duty, city-wise rate ranges, RBI rate norms). Gemini handles them too. The prompt structure matters more than the specific tool — our templates are tool-agnostic.
Does Ghar Ka Budget use AI to generate its calculator results?
No. Every Ghar Ka Budget calculator is a deterministic Indian-context formula running locally in your browser — based on CPWD SOR rates, MOSPI WPI, state stamp duty notifications, and primary government source data. AI is offered only as a verification layer for users who want a second opinion on the calculator's result. The calculator math is auditable in the page source and documented at our methodology page.
